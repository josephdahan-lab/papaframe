Most changes require a server restart to take effect (python3 server.py). Changing Photo folders also needs a photo-list rebuild — use the button below the form.

Photo Sync

Every night, new photos are downloaded from each source folder (e.g. on precision) to its destination on this frame, resized and saved as JPEG with their date and GPS kept. Then the photo list and the year / date / location filters are rebuilt. Photos removed from the source go to the trash below for you to confirm.

Folders

Trash

Photos that are no longer on the source. They are kept here until you delete them. Restoring puts a photo back on the frame, but the next sync trashes it again unless it is back on the source.
